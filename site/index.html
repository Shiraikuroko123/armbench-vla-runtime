下一项真正需要 GPU 的研究步骤，是加入第二个开放 VLA，并在任务对齐的同一异步协议下做多 seed、deadline 与扰动矩阵；真机结论仍需要 ROS2/驱动、标定和实体平台。The next GPU step is a second open VLA plus a task-aligned multi-seed deadline and perturbation matrix under one asynchronous protocol. Hardware claims still require ROS2/drivers, calibration, and a physical platform.
